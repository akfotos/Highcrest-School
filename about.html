Our Story

A caring place to learn, grow and thrive.

Highcrest School was registered with the Registrar General's Department in 2013 with the principal and sole objective of providing quality education to its patrons.

The school has six classrooms, a multipurpose room, a playground, on-site parking space for parents, and contemporary learning materials to enhance teaching and learning.

We are commencing with Ghana's Early Years' Curriculum, blended with Montessori and British Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) principles for early childhood development.
