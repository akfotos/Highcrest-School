Our Classrooms

Spaces where little learners can grow with confidence.

Every child is valued, supported, and encouraged to develop their heads, hearts, and hands.

A Place to Belong

A loving, disciplined environment for every stage.

We are on a mission to provide a loving and disciplined environment where each child is valued and encouraged to perform through the development of their heads, hearts and hands.

Up to 1 year

Babies Class

Our Babies Room is a homely, nurturing environment where our staff will love and care for your little ones as they begin their journey to grow.

Designed specifically for our very little ones, it is a safe environment perfectly suitable for babies and toddlers alike.

Ages 2 to 3

Toddlers Class

Our Toddlers Room provides a safe and inviting learning environment that enhances children's movement through the EYFS with play activities.

It is designed for children who are becoming adventurous and curious about their surroundings.

Up to age 5

Pre Schoolers

Our Pre Schoolers Room offers a stimulating, safe environment where older nursery children build confidence, play, enjoy learning, and get ready for school using phase one phonics.

The room is robustly designed for our more adventurous pupils up to the age of five.

Extra Care When You Need It

Flexible support for busy families.

We can engage your child on weekends or holidays when you are committed to other assignments.

Early Drop-off

From 6:00am

Aware of the traffic situation in Accra, we offer early morning drop-off for parents who need to get to work on time. Arrangements can be made for earlier drop-off times.

Late Pick-up

3:30pm to 8:30pm

Our After School Club keeps your child engaged until you return from work with activities including drawing, painting, music, coding, and more.

Admission in Progress

Applications are accepted throughout the year.

Flexible attendance options are available for parents working full-time, part-time, or seasonally. We have places for children from three months and upwards.
