Why Choose Highcrest School?

Early learning designed for every child to blossom.

We offer a blend of interactive early years learning programmes aimed at providing an excellent opportunity to improve your child's social and academic development.

Small Class Sizes

We are committed to keeping class sizes small so we can nurture and inspire every child to blossom.

Key Person

Each child is assigned a key worker who acts as their main carer, helping them feel safe and secure within a small group of children.

The key person gets to know your child and provides extra support where needed to help them reach their full potential.

Daily Learning Journal

We maintain a daily learning journal of every child's activity and development, providing regular updates to parents via email.

Parents can discuss their child's development and success whenever necessary with our friendly management team.
