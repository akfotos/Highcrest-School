About Us

Highcrest School

Highcrest School was registered with the Registrar General's Department in 2013 with the principal and sole objective of providing quality education to its patrons.

Our school has six classrooms, a multipurpose room, a playground, on-site parking space for parents, and contemporary learning materials to enhance teaching and learning.

Admissions
